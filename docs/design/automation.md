# Automation

Automation removes chores already mastered while preserving exploration, combat, rare resources, and decisions.

The Tidecatcher unlocks after crafting the Reinforced Heart, proving the player has gathered, fought, salvaged, and crafted manually. It is a free one-time construction choice at the workbench. Once active, it produces 1 wood every 2 seconds, stores at most 6, and transfers all stored wood automatically when approached. Production uses a deterministic accumulator independent of frame/update chunk size.
