# Endgame

Rifts combine modifiers, encounters, enemies, and build pressure with repeat rewards. Endless play must broaden combinations rather than only inflate enemy health.

The first rift unlocks after Abyssal Warden victory and runs three deterministic waves: two chasers; chaser plus Tide Slinger; Tide Slinger plus Stormcaller. Left shoulder / K enters near the portal, retreats while active, or exits after resolution. Player defeat safely fails and cleans the run. Completion drops a run-indexed rare Rift Cache; power improves for the first three runs and then caps at 9 rather than scaling forever.
