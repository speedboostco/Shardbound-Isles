# Resources

Resource nodes take discrete hits and provide distinct feedback. Destroyed drops are collected automatically at close range.

- The tree takes two hits and yields 3 wood, with Verdant/Emberglass island modifiers changing its yield.
- The stone outcrop at `(390, -80)` takes three hits, gains visible branching cracks after each impact, and yields 2 stone through a distinct gray-blue pickup.

Wood and stone remain separate authoritative HUD/save resources. Stone's first sink is the permanent Runed Whetstone workbench upgrade.
