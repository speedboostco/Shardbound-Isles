# Vertical Slice

The target slice covers movement, gathering, combat, procedural equipment, crafting and compact automation, installable island shards, a repeatable rift, versioned persistence, and controller-first UX.

## Current milestone

One arena proves movement, bounded camera, distinct tree/stone gathering and magnetic pickups, a contact chaser, a telegraphed ranged enemy, an elite tri-shot variation, a two-phase boss, a repeatable three-wave rift, one player attack, deterministic browsable equipment, three behaviorally distinct island-shard choices, the behavior-changing Riftwake Pulse legendary affix, controller-first equipment and world-building decisions, a two-recipe permanent-upgrade workbench, a Tidecatcher automating mastered wood gathering, schema-versioned local persistence with migration, a minimal HUD, and automated fixed-seed scenarios. Broader affix variety and production remain planned.
