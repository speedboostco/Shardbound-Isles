# Product Vision

Shardbound Isles is an offline single-player 2D action RPG where the player builds a character, a compact production base, and an archipelago. The defining promise is: **the world itself is loot**. Procedural island shards change resources, enemies, encounters, adjacency, risk, and reward.

The target is PC and Steam Deck, with controller-first play at 1280x800. Progress should regularly add power, a mechanic, a world opportunity, a production improvement, or a meaningful build decision.
