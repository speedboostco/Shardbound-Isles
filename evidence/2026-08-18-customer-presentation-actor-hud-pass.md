# Customer presentation actor and HUD pass

Date: 2026-08-18

## Outcome

The normal 1280x800 playfield is suitable for a customer-facing vertical-slice preview. Hero equipment now changes the visible character family immediately, the HUD occupies less of the world, controller actions replace keyboard/debug copy, and legacy procedural weapon/hit lines no longer compete with authored animation or bounded gameplay VFX.

This is a presentation milestone, not a claim that final shipping art or physical Steam Deck feel has been approved.

## Important changes

- Unarmed/gathering uses Blue Pawn.
- Melee uses Blue Warrior with authored sword animation.
- Ranged uses Blue Archer with authored bow animation.
- Magic uses Purple Pawn as the arcane conduit.
- `hero_animation_atlas.png` is a deterministic 1536x1280 atlas containing four non-overlapping 6x5 blocks of 128px cells.
- Every loadout exposes six non-empty idle, move, and attack frames. Unarmed and arcane deliberately use clean body clips for attack because the CC0 Pawn attack rows contain unrelated tools; authoritative impact VFX remain unchanged.
- Equipping or unequipping invalidates the cached hero texture and refreshes the silhouette in the same frame.
- Removed old procedural player attack beams/arcs and white hit strokes. Authored melee/bow poses, physical bow projectiles, attack profiles, and `GameplayVfx` now carry feedback.
- Normal HUD bounds changed from 457x347 to 392x286 pixels. Body type remains 16px or larger, the health bar remains 20px high, and the equipped family/item stays visible.
- Always-visible instructions now show controller actions only; keyboard bindings continue to work but are no longer customer-facing HUD copy.

## Known limitations

- Blue Archer has no dedicated walk row in this CC0 edition; its authored alternate idle row remains the locomotion fallback.
- Unarmed and arcane share clean body/casting timing rather than dedicated punch/staff attack art. Their actual impact type is distinguished through gameplay targeting, projectiles, color, and VFX.
- Logical north/south movement still shares a three-quarter pose; only left/right has an explicit sprite flip.
- The playfield is now appropriate for a vertical-slice preview, but final biome variety, bespoke protagonist identity, dedicated hit/death clips, audio feel, and marketing-grade capture remain later production work.
- No physical controller or Steam Deck playtest was performed in this environment.
