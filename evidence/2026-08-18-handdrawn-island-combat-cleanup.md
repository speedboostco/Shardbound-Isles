# Hand-drawn island and combat cleanup evidence

Date: 2026-08-18

## Outcome

- Removed full and near-full combat circles from player hit/wand feedback, Slime attacks, Ranger/elite telegraphs, and Abyssal Warden attacks. Directional wedges, aim lines, hit ticks, authored animation frames, and VFX remain.
- Rebuilt the hero runtime atlas with 16 clean transparent rows on the affected east/north locomotion cells. The source master is unchanged; the deterministic postprocessor owns the repair.
- Reduced Rift density from two enemies per wave to one and added a deterministic 2.75-second respite between waves.
- Replaced the repeating Emberwood grass-plus carpet with the selected Tiny Swords CC0 64px grass/path atlas, painted water backing, leaf-fringed boundaries, and sixteen bounded decoration placements.
- Added registered/import-validated terrain and decoration atlases plus exact CC0 provenance.

No new AI-generated bitmap was used in this pass. The runtime terrain supplement comes from the separately distributed Tiny Swords CC0 edition; the project-created Emberwood actors and interactive props remain unchanged.
